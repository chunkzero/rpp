RPP example pack — design notes (NOT shipped)
=============================================

These notes are excluded from the built pack by src/.rppignore (`*.txt`).

Palette
-------
- Diamond sword: pale blade (#d2dceb) on a wooden hilt.
- Magic gem: cyan diamond, lighter top-left, darker bottom-right.
- RPP bricks: staggered red-brick rows with light mortar.
- Ember: a 4-frame animated flame (see ember.png.mcmeta).

Build pipeline (see ../../rpp.config.ts)
-----------------------------------
1. json-minify     — compact every .json / .mcmeta.
2. mcmeta-validate — gate the build on valid pack.mcmeta + animations.
3. hash-rename     — fingerprint custom/ textures, emit rename_map.json.
Then the built-in squash pass minifies JSON, optimizes PNGs, and zips.
